What Can a Website Include?

A business website can include different pages and features based on its needs.

  • Home page with business information
  • About Us page
  • Services and products
  • Photo gallery
  • Contact form
  • Blog and news

Types of Websites

Business Websites

These websites help companies show their services and contact information.

Online Stores

Online stores help businesses show products and allow customers to shop online.

Personal Websites

Personal websites can show someone's skills, projects, and experience.
